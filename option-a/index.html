Professional Indian Relay League: Ancient Spirit. Relentless Speed.

A startup nonprofit league formed to organize and elevate Indian Relay Racing, “a sport of speed, courage, horses, family, and Indigenous tradition.”

Watch the film Scroll to run the exchange

How an exchange works

  1. The exchange

    The rider races one lap bareback, returns to the exchange area and leaps off.

  2. The next horse

    Then the rider mounts the next horse.

  3. Again

    The process repeats until all three horses have completed their laps.

Lap 1

The rider races Horse Three to the finish.

The fastest clean team wins, subject to official rules.

“Bareback riders and their teams sprint, leap between galloping horses, and exchange mounts at full speed in one of the most thrilling sports in North America.”

PIRL, on Mobridge 2026

Who it takes

Each Indian Relay team usually includes one rider, three horses, two holders, and a mugger, or catcher.

Divisions Planned

These divisions are planned and remain subject to Board approval.

  • Men's Premier

    The top tier of men's professional relay competition.

  • Women's Premier Relay

    Elite women's relay racing on the professional stage.

  • Men's Challenger

    A development tier and pathway toward the Premier division.

  • Women's Challenger

    A growing tier expanding opportunity in women's relay.

PIRL treats Women's Premier Relay as a major feature of the league story from the very beginning.

What PIRL is building Planned

  • Standardized Platform

    Consistent rules across events.

  • Safety, Welfare & Integrity

    Horse welfare and safety standards.

  • Premier Events

    Live events across North America.

  • Professional Media

    Streaming and broadcast coverage.

  • Bigger Purses

    Larger sponsorship and purse opportunities.

  • Championship Pathway

    “A transparent points and championship pathway that honors the best teams in the sport.”

No points, purses, or championship yet. The rulebook is under development.
