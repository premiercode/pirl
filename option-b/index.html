Watch the film How the sport works

A national platform for Indian Relay Racing: a sport of speed, courage, horses, family, and Indigenous tradition.

The exchange, in six frames

Each Indian Relay team usually includes one rider, three horses, two holders, and a mugger, or catcher.

The rider races one lap bareback, returns to the exchange area, leaps off, mounts the next horse, and repeats the process until all three horses have completed their laps.

The rider races Horse Three to the finish. The fastest clean team wins, subject to official rules.

Where it comes from

Horse culture among Native nations goes back centuries. The modern competitive sport developed in the 20th century. In PIRL’s words, Fort Hall is “a birthplace of modern competitive relay.”

“Bareback riders and their teams sprint, leap between galloping horses, and exchange mounts at full speed in one of the most thrilling sports in North America.”

Four planned divisions

These four divisions are planned and remain subject to Board approval.

  • Men’s Premier The top tier of men’s professional relay competition.
  • Men’s Challenger A development tier and pathway toward the Premier division.
  • Women’s Premier Relay Elite women’s relay racing on the professional stage.
  • Women’s Challenger A growing tier expanding opportunity in women’s relay.

PIRL treats Women’s Premier Relay as a major feature of the league story from the very beginning.

What PIRL is building

PIRL calls itself “an Indigenous-led sports property that can grow with integrity.”

Planned

PIRL is being structured with a smaller voting board, officers, committees, advisors, and a nonprofit parent framework.

  1. Standardized Platform

    Consistent rules across events.

  2. Safety, Welfare & Integrity

    Horse welfare and safety standards.

  3. Premier Events

    Live events across North America.

  4. Professional Media

    Streaming and broadcast coverage.

  5. Bigger Purses

    Larger sponsorship and purse opportunities.

  6. Championship Pathway

    “A transparent points and championship pathway that honors the best teams in the sport.”
